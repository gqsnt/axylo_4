The workloads are the plan's: P1 the `trend` fixture, P2 one series of `1x100000` and `1x1000000` typed points, P3 a 600-point window with 300 samples, P4 200 moves across `1x100000`, P5 24 `trend` charts, P7 `1x1000000` for owned buffers and 2,500 `trend` create–destroy cycles for the heap, P8 40 compact `trend` charts. The spec's P7 statement names 1,000 cycles; the harness runs 2,500 and compares the windows over cycles 1,500–2,000 and 2,000–2,500, as in the earlier reports.

### P1 against November 24

The November 24 figure of 19.64 ms comes from [`20261124T093822Z-first-trend-axylo-vs-uplot-5e7a`](../history/20261124T093822Z-first-trend-axylo-vs-uplot-5e7a.json): 10 complete pairs on the `stage1-fair-sota` artifact, software raster, the same Chrome version. Today's 19.31 ms is 0.33 ms lower, but the two runs used different artifacts and different pair counts, and other November 24 P1 records on that artifact measured 18.67 ms and 18.13 ms ([`…093701Z…-42e8`](../history/20261124T093701Z-first-trend-axylo-vs-echarts-42e8.json), [`…093741Z…-3792`](../history/20261124T093741Z-first-trend-axylo-vs-chartjs-tuned-3792.json)). This is an observed difference between two medians from different artifacts and sessions; it does not isolate session-to-session variation and is not a measured speed-up.
