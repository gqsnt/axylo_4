### What the grade establishes

The grader checks production console and handle diagnostics, detects an error state, and allows ordinary warnings and hints. It catches a bar substituted for a line, a shortened accessible name, and missing or swapped regional values. Requiring the requested title is justified even though A1 permits a generated fallback. A visually clipped title with an intact accessible name passes. [Grader](/home/canarit/projects/axylo/rehearsal/grade.mjs:122)

I interpret “reader criteria” as the applicable outcomes on the delivered chart. A1/A4 cover its name, caption, headers, units and values. F2/F4/F11 cover the *drawn* gaps, calendar days and order. F12 covers visible and accessible disclosure of omissions or changes. The grader checks only part of this. I directly verified that its comparator accepts visits labelled `Temperature (kg)` and rejects the correct date `06 April 2026`. It ignores time text after a recognized date. Its note check matches one English phrase, and every fixture expects no omission note. If agent code filters out a valid revenue row, the original-fixture comparison **does** fail, even though Axylo never saw the row and cannot disclose its loss.
