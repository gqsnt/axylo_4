**I would block release of the interaction layer at `a518cbb`.** A mouse click can select a different datum from the one under the pointer. Keyboard recovery from an empty zoom can leave the range saying “No data” over a populated plot. An all-zero donut exposes slice actions to the mouse that a keyboard cannot reach. The tooltip fallback can cover a control or extend outside the visible plot even when its placement function completes successfully.

I reviewed the named commit. The checkout was one commit ahead and had uncommitted browser-test changes, so I used the committed versions for the acceptance review. I created, edited and deleted no files. **Node** below means I exercised the production calculation or lookup with an in-memory fixture. **Trace** means I read the event, DOM or CSS path. The 555 passing Chrome acceptance tests are the supplied result. I did not run a browser, inspect Firefox or WebKit results, or observe speech.

**Decision versus WCAG.**

- **1.4.13 AA.** Pointer transparency is compatible with hoverability. A reader still needs a continuous route onto the shown content and the ability to move over it to read it under magnification. Testing one point just inside the near edge after a mandatory rest establishes less than that. Rules 3 and 3b also give opposing answers for a no-rest sweep through a scatter tooltip over another point. Specify when the hoverable presentation begins, provide persistence over its readable extent, and give activation unconditionally to the real pick. [W3C on content shown on hover or focus](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/content-on-hover-or-focus.html)
- **2.5.7 and 2.5.8 AA.** Following a corridor with no button down is hover movement. The relevant dragging operation is selecting a zoom range. Buttons provide ordinary zoom and pan, but cannot reproduce an arbitrary two-endpoint selection. Two clicks or editable endpoints would provide that function. Rule 4’s 24×24 minimum is appropriate for displayed row buttons. Measuring the CSS box alone does not establish that an unobscured target is available to operate. [W3C on dragging movements](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/dragging-movements.html) · [W3C on target size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html)
- **2.4.11 AA.** It concerns a focused component being entirely hidden by author content. A partial overlap of the datum ring alone is insufficient to establish that failure. A2 expressly protects the indicator, and rule 6 needs an explicit exclusion for focused controls and the actual focus strokes. The fallback in #4 can entirely cover a control. [W3C on focus not obscured](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html)
- **2.4.13 is AAA.** It measures qualifying changed pixels between focused and unfocused states. A 2 px token with good adjacent contrast does not establish it. For example, on a plain 300×200 empty-window proxy with only the inset plot outline, the ideal ink area between insets 1 and 3 is 1,968 px². The rectangular reference perimeter requires 2,000 px². A thicker inset outline and focused/unfocused pixel comparison would support an AAA claim. This is separate from the AA release verdict. [W3C on focus appearance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-appearance.html)
