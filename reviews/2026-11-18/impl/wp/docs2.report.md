### Per item

**1. Reading values.**
- `pages/accessibility.md` has a new section, "Reading values with a pointer":
  - The three readout modes: a floating tooltip at 240 × 140 px or more; a two-line strip under smaller plots; no readout when the strip would leave the plot under 80 px.
  - Placement order, and that the tooltip never covers the marks, a control or the focus ring.
  - The tooltip never takes the pointer. A rest of 250 ms holds the datum along the path to the tooltip.
  - A click or tap pins. Pinned rows and "+N more" are buttons of at least 24 px. A pin ends on Escape, a click elsewhere, a drag or pinch, or when its datum leaves the view. Nothing pins with `tooltip: false` or in no-readout mode.
- Escape row in the keyboard map: one thing per press, in the code's order (drag, Select range, tooltip/pin, single series, page), plus the rule for several charts on a page.
- Focus ring wording follows the new clarification.
- Also updated: the Tooltip row of `getting-started.md`, the pointer rows of `line/area/bar/scatter/donut.md`, and the `tooltip` row of `options.md`.

**2. Zoom, now including Select range.**
- `updating.md#zoom` was rewritten:
  - Zoom applies to date, time and number x on full charts only.
  - `zoom()` on a category axis, a donut or a compact chart reports AX150. It is printed and passed to `onDiagnostic`, but not kept in `chart.diagnostics`.
  - The four ways are listed: buttons with Pan left/right, a drag or the Select range toggle, keys and wheel, and pinch.
  - Select range follows `interaction.ts`: two clicks in either order, the first end marked, the toggle releases itself, and Escape, pressing it again or a drag ends it.
- Toolbar list and touch row in `accessibility.md`, Zoom rows of the chart pages, and `options.md` (`zoom` option and `zoom()` method) updated.
- `examples/zoom.html` now shows both forms of a zoom event's ends ('YYYY-MM-DD', or a number between two days).
- New check in `test/example-checks.ts`: press Select range, click 75% then 35% of the plot. The toggle releases, nothing is selected, and the view has between 2 and 30 points.
