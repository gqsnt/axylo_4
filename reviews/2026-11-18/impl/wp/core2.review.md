**Four defects block release.** A paused chart can replay rejected or superseded data. A sliding category window can select a different datum from the one announced. Zoom can remove every way to reset the chart. A focused slider can retain a maximum that prevents assistive technology from reaching newly arrived data.

**Ran** identifies results from the repository’s Node-compatible code. **Reasoned** identifies conclusions from tracing the DOM, controller, CSS, or tests. I did not launch a browser or screen reader.
