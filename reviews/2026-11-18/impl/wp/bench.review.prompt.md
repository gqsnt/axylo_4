You are an independent reviewer from a different provider than the author. Read-only: do not create, edit or delete files. Put everything in your final message. You cannot launch a browser in this sandbox; the statistics and analysis code runs in Node (`node --test bench/test/*.test.mjs`, and you may write throwaway scripts to stdout only). Say for each finding whether you ran something or reasoned it out.

Under review: `bench/` (`README.md`, `run.mjs`, `build.mjs`, `analyze.mjs`, `profile.mjs`, `lib/**`, `src/**`, `test/**`), the recorded measurements in `bench/history/` and `bench/results/` (in particular `bench/results/m1-renderer-comparison.md`), and the decision that rests on them, `docs/decisions/0002-mark-renderer.md`.

What it must do: `docs/04-plan-performance.md` (all of it: the measurement method, admission check, paired design, noise band, three outcomes, what makes a gain worth shipping, regression detection) and the budgets P1–P8 in `docs/02-product-spec.md` section 5. Phase 1's method problems, which this harness was built to avoid, are in `docs/01-landscape.md` section 5 and `reviews/2026-11-18/ledger.md`.

This harness will decide which optimisations ship and whether the canvas renderer is kept, so a flaw here costs more than a flaw in any one module. Find defects, most important first:

1. **Does each workload measure what its budget names?** For P1–P5, P7, P8: the start and end marks, what is inside and outside the timed region (data generation, adapter setup, style and layout, paint, the frame after), whether "complete" is established or assumed, whether the accessibility layer is on, whether the build measured is the production build, whether the streaming clock is independent of the library's work, whether pointer latency is measured to the frame that shows the readout. Name every workload a budget needs that does not exist.
2. **Statistics.** The paired bootstrap (resampling unit, number of resamples, interval type, seed handling), the A-versus-A noise band and how it is used, the admission check (what it rejects and whether a bad machine state can pass), block balancing (AB/BA order, warm-up, carry-over between adapters in one page versus fresh pages), the three-outcome rule, multiple comparisons when many workloads are judged at once, medians versus tails. Construct inputs where the analysis gives a confident wrong answer and run them.
3. **Fairness between libraries.** Same data, size, device pixel ratio, fonts, animation settings; what "default options" means for each adapter and whether the tuned variants are declared; whether uPlot and Chart.js are asked to do less (no accessibility layer, no tooltip) and whether the write-up says so; the no-op adapter's role.
4. **The renderer comparison and decision 0002.** Does the recorded data support "SVG by default"? Which conclusions rest on software rasterisation in headless Chrome and would not transfer to a GPU-backed browser? Which workloads that could reverse the decision were not measured (dense scatter, many series, resize, theme switch, 1,000,000 points, pan and zoom)? Is anything in the write-up stated more strongly than its intervals allow?
5. **Regression detection.** The history format, what is compared with what, how a regression is told from machine drift, what happens when Chrome or the machine changes, whether a slow drift across many small accepted changes is caught.
6. **Profiles.** Whether `profile.mjs` attributes time correctly (self versus total, inlined frames, GC, minified names in a production build) and whether the profile summaries in `bench/results/profiles/` support the claim that normalisation and garbage collection, not drawing, dominate at 100,000 points.
7. **Tests that do not test**, and code paths in `analyze.mjs` and `lib/` with no test.

For each finding: severity (B invalidates a conclusion already drawn, M major, m minor), file and line, the concrete input or sequence, what goes wrong, and the fix. At most 18 findings, most severe first. No praise. End with one line per area you examined closely and found sound, and a short list: which statements in `m1-renderer-comparison.md` and decision 0002 you would keep, weaken or withdraw.
